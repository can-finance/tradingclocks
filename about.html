About

What is Trading Clocks?

Trading Clocks is a free dashboard for tracking when the world's stock markets are open. Each exchange gets a live clock in its own timezone with an open/closed status and a countdown to the next open or close. It accounts for weekends, public holidays, early-close days, lunch breaks, and daylight saving time transitions.

Alongside the main dashboard there is a daily market hours schedule, a DST transition reference, and holiday calendars for 2026 and 2027.

Coverage

The site covers major stock exchanges across the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, including every MSCI Developed Market: from NYSE and Nasdaq through the European exchanges (LSE, Euronext, XETRA, SIX, and others) to Tokyo, Hong Kong, Sydney, and beyond. Coverage is limited to cash equity markets.

Where the data comes from

Trading hours and holiday calendars are sourced directly from each exchange's own official publications.

Exchanges typically publish the following year's holiday calendar during the current year. Information here is updated as exchanges publish official times and holidays, never from unconfirmed dates. The 2027 holidays page marks each market as fully updated, partially updated, or still awaiting official dates, so you can see exactly which calendars are confirmed.

Daylight saving time is handled by the browser's built-in IANA timezone database, not by hand-maintained dates, so clocks stay correct through every DST transition automatically.

Spotted an error?

If a market's hours, holidays, or status look wrong, please tell us; corrections are genuinely appreciated and usually applied quickly. Email info@tradingclocks.com with the market and the date in question.

The stock exchange hours and holiday schedules provided on this site are for informational purposes only. While believed to be accurate, TradingClocks.com makes no guarantees regarding the correctness or completeness of the data. Please confirm official trading hours directly with the respective exchanges before making any trading decisions.
